# Design history — four physical versions

The sequence below is reconstructed from dated CAD and print/fit records,
anchored by the project owner's descriptions of versions one and four. The
physical sequence is well supported; these numbers are editorial labels, not
literal archive revision names.

## Version one: multi-piece upright chimney enclosure (Sep 4–6)

Two Sparks stand on edge in modular cells connected to a rear chimney with a
top exhaust fan. Separate lids, caps, ties, hood, and cable plates make a
multi-piece assembly. It was glued up and the pieces and gaps proved
unwieldy. A specification dated September 4 describes the cells/chimney
layout; print records show all parts for the two-Spark build complete on
September 6 with the assembly glued that evening. A follow-up brief called
that two-cell build "too big". The older front-push rack proposals are
precursor designs, not additional confirmed physical builds.

## Version two: compact upright box with a bottom plate (Sep 6–9)

The pair remains upright, but a single main shell and removable bottom plate
replace the many-part rack. The fan sits inside under the roof. Honeycomb
openings appear in the roof and sides. Design records date the cup part
complete September 8 (plate same evening), with fit feedback the same day —
openings 6 mm too short, inaccessible controls/cable issues, loose fit — and
the move to a stacked arrangement recorded on September 9. This establishes a
distinct printed design between the first modular enclosure and the stacked
case.

## Version three: first stacked front-fan case, with a tray (Sep 9–11)

Two Sparks lie flat, one above the other, behind a front fan, with an open
rear. A middle tray supports the upper device. Early retention uses bezel
hooks and a friction-fit rear bar, followed by screw-retention revisions and
attempts to fix the tray. The first four-part print set and September 10 fit
feedback are on record; a September 11 fit report says the tray stack was
2–3 mm too thick and one Spark's bottom cover had to be removed to fit the
second machine — that workaround belongs to this build, not to the final
design.

## Version four: no tray, reinforced side supports, positive retention (Sep 10–15) — this repo

C-channel side supports replace the center tray. Weight-bearing ledges gain
reinforcement (rev 6b). Screws secure the bezel and retaining bar (rev 5).
The later rear-bar revision (rev 8) adds seven 20 x 15 mm windows while
retaining the lips that hold the computers in place.

Dated milestones: tray removal decided September 10; reinforced rev 6b
geometry September 11; first completed black set September 12 ("turned out
perfect"); white set with the vented rev 8 bar complete September 13; a
second black set complete September 15. Three version-four cases were built,
each holding two Sparks. The vented bar is a refinement within this final
design family, not a fifth version.

## Remaining limits

The September 9 cooling test (see [THERMAL.md](THERMAL.md)) predates the
first stacked build, and the test records do not identify which of the two
earlier enclosures was installed. Photo-to-build mapping for the earlier
photos is inferred from visible geometry, not filenames.
